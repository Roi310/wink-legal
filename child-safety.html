Nudge Blush — Child Safety Standards

Nudge Blush has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). These are our published standards against CSAE.

Adults only

Nudge Blush is strictly for adults aged 18 and over. Every new account must provide a date of birth at signup, and anyone under 18 is blocked from using the app.

Zero tolerance

Any content or behavior involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child results in an immediate and permanent ban from Nudge Blush. We do not allow child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind on our service — real or AI-generated.

Reporting

If you encounter anything that may involve the exploitation of a child:

  • Use the Report option on the user's profile in the app — every report is reviewed.
  • Or email us directly at support@nudgeblush.com.

Reports involving child safety are treated as our highest priority.

Cooperation with authorities

We comply with all applicable child safety laws. Where required, we report suspected child sexual abuse material to the relevant regional and national authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

Contact

For questions about these standards: support@nudgeblush.com.
