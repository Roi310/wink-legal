Privacy Policy for Nudge

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Who we are: Nudge is operated by Andrew Maunger, a sole proprietor doing business as Nudge. Contact: amaunger@hotmail.com.

Nudge is a dating app for adults (18+) designed for people who find approaching others difficult. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use Nudge.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your mobile phone number (used to verify your identity) and date of birth (used to confirm you are 18 or older).
  • Profile information: display name, photos, bio, and preferences you choose to add.
  • Location (fuzzy only): Nudge shows you people nearby using approximate proximity (such as "within 1 mile"). Your precise GPS coordinates never leave your phone — they are converted on-device into a coarse geographic area before anything is sent to our servers.
  • Messages: chat messages are exchanged only after two people mutually match. Messages are private between the participants.
  • Device information: push notification token (so we can notify you of matches and messages), device type, and app version.
  • Reports: if you report another user, we receive the report details to review for safety.
  • Anonymous usage analytics: we collect anonymous events about how the app is used (for example: signup completed, a nudge was sent, a match was created, the app encountered an error). These events are not linked to your phone number or identity, and they never include your location, messages, or photos. Analytics is provided by PostHog Inc.

2. SMS verification messages

When you sign up or sign in, Nudge sends a one-time verification code by SMS to the phone number you enter:

  • Consent: by entering your phone number and tapping "Send code" in the app, you consent to receive this verification text.
  • Message type: transactional verification codes only. We never send marketing or promotional texts.
  • Frequency: messages are sent only when you request a code (signup or sign-in).
  • Opt-out: reply STOP to any message to opt out of SMS. Reply HELP for help.
  • Rates: message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier.
  • Provider: SMS delivery is handled by Twilio Inc. on our behalf.
  • Non-sharing: your mobile number will not be shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes. It is used only for account verification as described in this section. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account (phone verification, age check).
  • To show you nearby people and enable matching and chat.
  • To send account-related notifications (matches, messages) — never advertising.
  • To keep Nudge safe: reviewing abuse reports and preventing fake accounts.
  • To improve the app and fix problems.

4. What we share — and what we never share

  • We never sell your personal information.
  • Service providers: our backend is hosted by Supabase (data storage), SMS is delivered by Twilio, and anonymous usage analytics is provided by PostHog. They process data only to provide these services.
  • Other users: see only your public profile (name, photos, bio, approximate proximity) — never your phone number, date of birth, or precise location.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect safety.

5. Your choices and controls

  • Delete your account: in the app under Settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, photos, messages, matches, and account data from our servers.
  • Push notifications: turn them off in your device settings at any time.
  • Location: Nudge only works with approximate location; you can revoke location permission in device settings (nearby discovery will stop working).
  • Biometric app lock: can be turned on/off in Settings.

6. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our live systems. Backups may retain residual copies for a limited time before being overwritten.

7. Security

We use technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections, screenshot and screen-recording blocking inside chats, and an optional biometric app lock. No method is 100% secure, and screenshots taken with a separate device cannot be prevented.

8. Children

Nudge is for adults 18 and older only. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and underage accounts are removed.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Nudge evolves. Material changes will be announced in the app. Continued use after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: amaunger@hotmail.com.
