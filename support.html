Common questions

How does Nudge Blush work?
Turn on Nudge Mode to become visible to people nearby. Send a private Nudge to someone you're interested in — they'll never know unless they nudge you back. When it's mutual, chat unlocks.

Does anyone see my exact location?
No. Your location is always fuzzy — other people only ever see an approximate distance, never where you actually are.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings in the app and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, photos, and messages.

Someone is making me uncomfortable. What do I do?
You can block any user from their profile or inside a chat. To report a user, use the Report option on their profile — every report is reviewed.

Why do I need to verify my phone number?
Phone verification keeps the community real and keeps bots out. One number equals one account.
